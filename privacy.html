Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Minvo keeps your goals, your words and your progress on your iPhone. The only thing we collect is anonymous usage data, so we can see how the app is used and make it better. We never sell it, never use it for ads, and never use it for anything else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your voice and what you say. When you talk to Pip, speech is turned into text on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio and the words are never sent to us.
  • Your goals and progress. The words of your goals, check-ins, small wins, time earned and used, seeds, streaks, your tree and your garden are saved on your iPhone.
  • App usage timing. Minvo notes when your locked apps are opened, to learn your routine. This stays on your iPhone and is not included in backups.
  • Screen Time. Minvo uses Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple gives Minvo private tokens for the apps you choose, not their names or what you do in them.

Anonymous usage data we collect

To learn what works and what doesn't, Minvo sends a small amount of usage data:

  • Which screens and setup steps you reach, and how long each step takes.
  • When you check in, whether you spoke or typed, and whether Pip understood. Never what you said.
  • How many goals you have, and their general shape: how often they repeat, their reward time, whether they help your tree, and a broad topic such as exercise or study that your iPhone works out. Never the words of your goals.
  • How often you open the app.
  • The app version, iOS version, device model and language.
  • A random ID made for this install, so we can tell when someone comes back. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple account, and a new one is made if you reinstall.

We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, advertising ID, or anything about the other apps you use.

This data is processed by TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, only to give us usage reports. We use it only to understand and improve Minvo. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it to track you across other apps and websites.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup, iOS may include Minvo's saved progress in your own backup. That backup belongs to your Apple account; we can't see it.

Notifications

Goal reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Children

Minvo doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this policy first and tell you in the app.
