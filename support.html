Common questions

A locked app opened without the blocker

If you tapped "Use 5 minutes", the app stays open until you've used it for 5 minutes in total. The blocker comes back after that.

Minvo can't lock apps

Open Minvo, go to You, then Help & privacy, then Screen Time, and reconnect. Screen Time access has to stay on in Settings.

Pip doesn't hear me

Check that Minvo can use the microphone and speech recognition in Settings, Privacy & Security.

Where do seeds come from?

Time left in your Time Jar at the end of the day turns into seeds: 5 minutes kept is 1 seed.

Can I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your iPhone. Deleting Minvo deletes it.

Minvo needs iOS 26.5 or later.
